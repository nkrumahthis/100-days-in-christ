# 100 In Christ - 2026

## Introduction

**What This Is**

This is a 100-day journey into the consciousness of who you are in Christ. Not who you're trying to become. Not who you should be. Who you already are, right now, because of what Christ has finished.

This guide is built on one foundational truth: **Your sin is not a barrier between you and God.** God's love for you is unchanging. God's stance toward you is settled. When you sin, you separate yourself from His presence through darkness—but He never moves away from you. He calls you back. And restoration is immediate and complete.

**Who This Is For**

These teachings are designed for young women in their early 30s who are hungry to understand their identity in Christ at a deeper level. Not information that sounds nice. Truth that actually rewires how you see yourself, how you approach God, and how you move through the world.

**How It Works**

Each day presents one clear truth about who you are in Christ, supported by Scripture. The session is structured for 1 hour:
- 10 minutes: Opening prayer
- 40 minutes: Teaching/study of the day's truth
- 10 minutes: Closing prayer

The content is designed to land in 30 minutes of solid teaching, with 10 minutes for questions or reflection.

**The Structure**

The 100 days are organized into sections, each building on the last:

**Section 1: Sin Dealt With (Days 1-10)** — What Christ has finished for you
- Your forgiveness is complete
- Your righteousness is established  
- God's love doesn't change based on your sin
- You have authority over sin
- You are a new creation
- You are holy

**Section 2: Peace & Reconciliation with God (Days 11-20)** — Who you are and how you relate to God
- You have peace with God (the end of hostility)
- You are adopted—chosen and beloved
- You have direct access to God
- Your prayers have first-class treatment
- You are an insider; God does His work through you
- God sees you as capable and honors you
- You speak boldly to Him and for Him
- When you separate yourself through sin, He calls you back
- God's punishment fell on Christ; consequences are real but not punitive
- Grace super-abounds

*[Sections 3-10 continue to build the full consciousness of your identity in Christ]*

**The Central Truth**

Throughout these 100 days, one message keeps landing:

**You are completely loved. Your sin does not separate you from God's love. God is not mad at you. God is not disappointed in you. God is not waiting for you to be good enough. You are accepted, fully, right now, because Christ finished the work.**

When you sin, God doesn't move away. You create distance through darkness (unforgiveness, hardness, hiding). But God stands ready to restore you the moment you confess and come back into the light.

Your job is not to earn God's approval. Your job is to believe you have it—because you do—and to live from that reality.

**The Goal**

By the end of 100 days, you will have built a consciousness so settled in who you are in Christ that:
- Shame loses its power
- Condemnation becomes a voice you don't listen to
- You approach God as a beloved child, not a servant trying to earn favor
- You move through the world with the quiet confidence of someone who belongs to God
- You understand that your choices matter (not because God is punishing you, but because natural consequences are real)
- You live from grace, not from fear

**A Note on Perfect Tense**

Throughout this guide, you'll see the language of perfect tense: *has been finished, have been forgiven, are made righteous, have been redeemed.* This is intentional. These are not things God is working toward. These are completed facts that happened at the cross and remain your reality now. Your job is to believe them and live accordingly.

## Section 1: Sin Dealt With: Sin / Righteousness / Justification

recommended reading: Romans

### Day 1 - You are forgiven

**Main Scripture: Colossians 1:14**

"In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins…"

Notice the tense: "we have" — present possession of a completed act.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

1 John 2:12 — "I write unto you, little children, because your sins are forgiven you for his name's sake." Direct address. Not "will be" or "can be." *Are forgiven.*

Colossians 2:13 — "And you, being dead in your sins… hath he quickened together with him, having forgiven you all trespasses." All. Not some. All.

Romans 8:1 — "There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus." No condemnation in Christ. This is your legal standing.

Hebrews 10:17 — "Their sins and iniquities will I remember no more." God's memory of your sin is deleted. He does not store it.

**Reality to Master:**

Your sins are forgiven. Not some. Not if you're good enough. Not pending. **All trespasses are forgiven.** This is not a feeling—it's a fact that happened at the cross and remains your legal status before God forever.

More importantly: **God's love toward you does not change based on your sin.** He is not withholding forgiveness waiting for you to deserve it. He is not holding a record in secret. His stance toward you is settled. He is not angry at you. He does not move away from you based on what you've done.

God does not visit consequences of your sin on you (nature does; God stands on your team to help you through). In the spirit, you do not appear with sin on you. Nobody in heaven can tell you've ever sinned.

**Important Notes:**

The hardest part of forgiveness is believing *it's that complete* and that *God's heart toward you never changes*. We're trained to believe consequences must be paid, records must be kept, people must remember, and love must be earned. But God says: deleted. Forgotten. No record. And His affection toward you is the same whether you sin or not.

This is the foundation for all that follows—if you cannot believe God's love is unchanging, you cannot walk in righteousness, access, or power. You'll be trying to earn something already freely given.

Young women often carry secret shame or think certain sins are "too big" to be forgiven, or they believe God is secretly disappointed. This is a lie. The blood covers everything. God is not secretly mad at you.

### Day 2 - Grace for the weak

**Main Scripture: Romans 5:20**

"Moreover the law entered that the offence might abound. But where sin abounded, grace did much more abound."

Where sin is great, grace is *greater*. Grace is not sparse. It is abundant.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

2 Corinthians 12:9 — "And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness." God's power doesn't activate *after* you get strong. It activates *in* your weakness.

Romans 5:8 — "But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us." Christ came when you were powerless, not after you became worthy.

Hebrews 4:15–16 — "For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities… Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need." Jesus understands weakness because He took on humanity. And because of that, we approach boldly—not shamefully.

Romans 8:26 — "Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities…" The Spirit doesn't just empower strength; He helps your weakness in real time.

**Reality to Master:**

The pattern of grace is not "get strong, then God will accept you." The pattern is "come weak, and receive transforming grace." You don't have to pretend to be fine. You don't have to fix yourself first. 

Grace is not thin or measured. Where you are most broken, grace abounds more. The greater your need, the greater His sufficiency. And this grace comes with **no shift in God's affection toward you**—He is not disgusted by your weakness. He is not disappointed you're not stronger. His love for you in weakness is exactly the same as His love for you when you're strong.

**Important Notes:**

This matters enormously for young women who've been taught (formally or by culture) that God helps those who help themselves, or that you need to "get it together" before you can be spiritual. That's a lie rooted in works, not grace. The moment you're aware you can't, you're in the exact position for grace to work.

God doesn't turn away from weakness. He leans toward it. He meets you there. His stance toward you doesn't change based on how strong or broken you are.

### Day 2 - You have no record of sins

**Main Scripture: Hebrews 10:17**

"Their sins and iniquities will I remember no more."

God does not have you in a file marked "Former Sinner." He will not recall it. Not because He forgets—He's omniscient. But because He *chooses* to remember no more.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Isaiah 43:25 — "I, even I, am he that blotteth out thy transgressions for mine own sake… and will not remember thy sins." Blotted out. Erased. Not filed away.

Psalm 103:3 — "Who forgiveth all thine iniquities… [and] heals all thy diseases." Forgiveness and healing are paired—same source, same completeness.

Micah 7:19 — "Thou wilt cast all their sins into the depths of the sea." Not filed. Not stored. Cast away, sunk, unretrievable.

Isaiah 38:17 — "Thou hast cast all my sins behind thy back." Behind Him. Out of His view. Out of His reckoning.

1 John 1:9 — "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness." When you confess, you're not informing God of something He didn't know; you're aligning with what's already true.

**Reality to Master:**

Your sin record is blank before God. Not "in progress." Not "pending review." Not "you're on probation." **Blank.** This is your standing eternally.

This doesn't mean consequences disappear (natural law still operates). It means **God is not against you. He is not holding anything over your head. He has not filed you away under "sinner who needs to prove herself."** He chooses to remember your sin no more. His relationship with you is not based on a record He's keeping. You do not appear to heaven with a stain on you, and He does not see you that way either.

**Important Notes:**

Satan's primary tool is accusation: "God will never forgive that," "You're disqualified," "He's holding this against you," "He's secretly keeping score." But the scripture is clear: God chooses to remember your sin no more. His stance toward you is not conditional on your behavior. When accusation comes, the answer isn't self-defense or self-improvement—it's the blood. The record is gone. God is not holding it. He has not moved away.

### Day 4 - The divine exchange

**Main Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:21**

"For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him."

This is *the* exchange. God took your sin and placed it on Jesus. Then He took Jesus' righteousness and placed it on you. The mechanics: substitution. The timing: completed. The scope: total.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Isaiah 53:6 — "All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the Lord hath laid on him the iniquity of us all." The Lord *laid* it on Him. Not suggested. Not offered. *Laid.*

1 Peter 2:24 — "Who his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree, that we, being dead to sins, should live unto righteousness." He bore them. He carried them. We live unto righteousness as a result.

Galatians 3:13 — "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us." The exchange pattern repeated: He became the curse so you could be redeemed.

Romans 4:25 — "Who was delivered for our offences, and was raised again for our justification." Delivered for our offences. Raised for our justification. The exchange is complete and verified by the resurrection.

**Reality to Master:**

You did not keep your sin. Jesus took it. You did not keep your shame. Jesus took it. You did not keep the curse. Jesus took it.

In exchange, you received what Jesus has: righteousness, acceptance, blessing, redemption. This is not a loan. It's a permanent exchange. **God does not take it back.** Even if you sin, your righteousness in Christ is not revoked. It's not conditional. It's not fragile.

Your job is not to become righteous. Your job is to *believe you are righteous* and live accordingly. Your standing with God is not dependent on your next choice. It is established in Christ.

**Important Notes:**

Many believers think: "I sin, God forgives, I'm back to zero." But that's not the exchange. The exchange is: "I sin, Jesus took it, I receive His righteousness." You don't go back to zero; you go to *positive*—you're credited with Christ's standing, not just cleared of debt. Your righteousness isn't revoked when you stumble.

This is what makes boldness possible. You're not approaching God as a former sinner trying to be good. You're approaching Him as someone wearing Jesus' righteousness—and that doesn't come off.

### Day 5 - You are justified

**Main Scripture: Romans 5:18–19**

"Therefore as by the offence of one judgment came upon all men to condemnation; even so by the righteousness of one the free gift came upon all men unto justification of life... For as by one man's disobedience many were made sinners, so by the obedience of one shall many be made righteous."

One act. Adam's disobedience made all sinners. Christ's obedience made all righteous. You are justified by His obedience, not by your effort.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Romans 3:28 — "Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law." Not by what you do. By faith in what He did.

Romans 4:25 — "Who was delivered for our offences, and was raised again for our justification." The resurrection is God's signature on your justification. It's settled.

Galatians 2:16 — "Knowing that a man is not justified by the works of the law, but by the faith of Jesus Christ, even we have believed in Jesus Christ, that we might be justified by the faith of Christ, and not by the works of the law." Not your works. His faithfulness.

1 Corinthians 1:30 — "But of him are ye in Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom, and righteousness, and sanctification, and redemption." Christ is your righteousness. You are justified *in Him*.

Titus 3:7 — "That being justified by his grace, we should be made heirs according to the hope of eternal life." Justified by grace. Made heirs. This is your legal inheritance.

**Reality to Master:**

To be justified means to be *declared righteous*. Not made righteous through effort. Declared. Acquitted. Accepted. This happened at the cross and **stands forever. The verdict cannot be overturned.**

You are not in a perpetual courtroom trying to defend yourself or re-argue your case. The verdict is in. You are justified. God looks at you and sees not a sinner, but someone who has Christ's righteousness credited to her account. That ruling doesn't change based on your next decision.

**Important Notes:**

Justification is not about *feeling* righteous. It's about being *declared* righteous by God. A young woman may feel shame, insecurity, or unworthiness—and that's normal human emotion. But her standing before God is not based on her feelings. It's based on the verdict: justified. And verdicts, once rendered, do not get reversed.

Even if you stumble, your justification doesn't get un-declared. God is not reopening the case.

### Day 6 - You are righteous

**Main Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:21**

"For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him."

You are not *becoming* righteous. You *are* righteous. Present tense. Now. In Christ.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Romans 5:17 — "For if by one man's offence death reigned by one; much more they which receive abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ." You receive righteousness as a *gift*. Not earned. Given.

Romans 5:19 — "For as by one man's disobedience many were made sinners, so by the obedience of one shall many be made righteous." Made righteous. Past tense. Done.

1 Corinthians 1:30 — "But of him are ye in Christ Jesus, who of God is made unto us wisdom, and righteousness, and sanctification, and redemption." Christ is made your righteousness. You don't have to produce it.

Philippians 3:9 — "And be found in him, not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith." Your righteousness is not yours—it's Christ's, credited to you.

**Reality to Master:**

Righteousness means being *in right standing with God*. Not perfect behavior. Not sinless performance. Right standing. You have it. God accepts you. Not because you're good enough, but because Christ's righteousness is yours. **This acceptance is not conditional on your next choice.**

God doesn't look at you and see your failures, your past, your shame. He looks at you and sees the righteousness of Christ. That is what He accepts. He accepts you based on what Christ did, not on what you do.

**Important Notes:**

This is where boldness is born. A young woman who truly believes she's righteous—not someday, but now—will not approach God or people from shame. She'll approach from rest. She won't be striving to prove herself or trying to stay on God's good side. She'll be confident in who she already is.

The enemy works hard to make you doubt this. He'll whisper: "You're not righteous—look at what you did," or "You were righteous but you blew it." But righteousness is not a grade you earn or lose. It's a gift you've been given, based on Christ, and Christ doesn't change.

### Day 7 - You can say no to sin

**Main Scripture: Romans 6:6–7**

"Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin. For he that is dead is freed from sin."

Your old self was co-crucified with Christ. The body of sin—the whole system that served sin—was destroyed. You are freed from sin's dominion. This happened. Past tense. It is done.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Romans 6:11 — "Likewise reckon ye also yourselves to be dead indeed unto sin, but alive unto God through Jesus Christ our Lord." Count yourself dead to sin's power and alive to God's power.

Galatians 2:20 — "I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me." Your old identity died. You've been raised with a new identity.

Colossians 2:14–15 — "Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us... And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it." The certificate of your debt is gone. The powers that had leverage are stripped.

Romans 6:18 — "Being then made free from sin, ye became the servants of righteousness." You've been freed. Free is your actual condition.

James 1:14–15 — "But every man is tempted, when he is drawn away of his own lust, and enticed. Then when lust hath conceived, it bringeth forth sin: and sin, when it is finished, bringeth forth death." Sin is a progression: temptation → consent → action → consequence. You can interrupt it at consent.

**Reality to Master:**

Sin is a power, not just an action. It will attempt to exert dominion over you. But it has *no rightful power* over you anymore. You were freed.

You don't have to obey. You can say no. You won't die—urges come and fade; you'll survive. But the *consequences* of what you do with the urge outlive the urge itself. So the real question isn't "Can I?" It's "What happens next?"

Here's what's crucial: **When you sin, God doesn't withdraw His love or change His stance toward you.** But you separate yourself from His presence through darkness (unforgiveness, hardness, refusing to come into the light). God stands ready to restore that connection the moment you're willing to confess and come back to the light (1 John 1:7, 1:9).

When temptation comes, remember: you died with Christ. That old system doesn't own you. You have a new master—righteousness.

**Important Notes:**

Young women often think: "I'll always struggle with this sin. It's just who I am." But you were someone else. That person was crucified. You're new. The old patterns may knock on the door (conditioned responses, habit pathways), but they don't have legal claim on you.

This doesn't mean sin becomes easy to resist. It means sin doesn't have *authority* over you. There's a difference. Authority comes from a lie you believe. Once you know the truth, authority breaks.

Satan told you: "You're enslaved to this." Christ showed you: "You're freed." And here's the other part: when you do sin, Satan tells you, "God is disappointed; you're disqualified now." That's also a lie. God's door is open. He's not the one who left. You were.

### Day 8 - You are free from the fear of death

**Main Scripture: Romans 6:9**

"Knowing that Christ being raised from the dead dieth no more; death hath no more dominion over him."

You are free from the fear of death. Death no longer has dominion. You already died — in Christ. We are dead. So we do not have the same limits that those who are not dead live under.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Hebrews 9:27 — "It is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment." Once. The appointment has been kept. You died in Christ. The death that was coming for you—you already went through it. The debt is paid. The sentence is served. The appointment is closed.

Romans 8:1 — "There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus." After death, judgment. We have died and have been judged. The verdict came back: acquitted.

Romans 8:33 — "Who shall bring a charge against God's elect? It is God who justifies." The enemy cannot bring a charge that sticks. Dead. Judged. Acquitted. That is your legal standing before the entire spiritual order.

2 Corinthians 5:10 — "We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive what is due for things done in the body." A future appearance remains—not for condemnation, but for account. Paul includes himself. This is the bema.

**Reality to Master:**

We are not afraid of satan because the worst he can do is already done to us. You cannot threaten someone who has already been killed. You cannot negotiate with a woman who needs nothing you can offer.

We are not afraid of death because we are certain we are not at risk of damnation. We are actually excited to see God because we have already passed through judgment. Condemnation is finished in Christ.

When fear comes whispering ("What if you die? What if you're judged? What if you're not enough?"), the answer is: "I have already died. I have already been judged. I have already been acquitted. There is nothing left to fear."

**Important Notes:**

The structure is two-fold: **Condemnation**—already judged, already acquitted, finished in Christ. **Stewardship**—still to be rendered, motivating faithful living now.

The bema seat (2 Corinthians 5:10) is not a courtroom. The Greek word *bema* is the raised platform at the end of a Greek athletic competition where the judge stood to distribute prizes to the winners. Paul's audience understood this immediately. It is not a criminal tribunal. It is an awards ceremony.

1 Corinthians 3:12–15 maps this out: work tested by fire. Some survives—gold, silver, precious stones. Some burns—wood, hay, stubble. But the verdict on the one whose work burns: "he himself will be saved, but only as through fire." Even the one with nothing to show is still saved. The question at the bema is never *are you in*—that is settled. The question is *what did you build while you were there*.

The crowns are not metaphors for vague spiritual feelings. They are specific rewards for specific kinds of faithfulness:

- The **incorruptible crown** — for disciplined living (1 Corinthians 9:25)
- The **crown of righteousness** — for those who love His appearing (2 Timothy 4:8)
- The **crown of life** — for enduring trial and temptation (James 1:12, Revelation 2:10)
- The **crown of rejoicing** — for souls won (1 Thessalonians 2:19)
- The **crown of glory** — for faithful shepherding (1 Peter 5:4)

So picture the arc: You came to earth. You lived in a body. You died in Christ—death and judgment already settled, condemnation gone. You arrive at the marriage feast of the Lamb—the great celebration Scripture has been building toward since Genesis. As part of that celebration your earthly work is reviewed and rewarded. Not to shame you. Not to punish you. To honour what was done in faithfulness.

You are not only unkillable—you are already cleared. Walk in that.

### Day 9 - You are a new creation—new identity, new person

**Main Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17**

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

You are not improved. You are not reformed. You are *new*. The old person—her failures, her story, her shame—is passed away. You are not that person anymore.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Ephesians 4:22–24 — "That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; And be renewed in the spirit of your mind; And that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness." You have a new self, created in righteousness and holiness.

Colossians 3:9–10 — "Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds; And have put on the new man, which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him." The old man is *put off*—removed like old clothes. The new man is *put on*.

John 1:12–13 — "But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name: Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God." You were born again. Different parentage. Different nature.

1 Peter 1:23 — "Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever." You have an incorruptible nature now—not based on flesh, genetics, or family patterns.

1 John 3:1–2 — "Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God... Now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him." You are a daughter of God. Now. Not becoming. Now.

**Reality to Master:**

Your old identity is finished. The person who was bound by shame, driven by sin, ruled by fear—that person does not exist anymore. She died and rose new.

This doesn't mean your *memories* disappear or your *history* rewrites. It means your *identity* changes. You are no longer "that girl who did those things." You are a daughter of God, born of His nature, bearing His image. This identity is not shaken or changed by your sins. When you sin, you haven't reverted to who you were. You are still new; you're just walking in darkness instead of light.

When the enemy whispers your old story ("Remember what you were? You're still that."), the answer is: "That was her. I'm new. And even when I stumble, I'm still new—I just need to come back to the light."

**Important Notes:**

This is the hardest thing to believe for women with heavy pasts. The mind says, "But I remember what I did. I can't just be new." But being new doesn't erase memory—it reframes identity. You can remember something and not *be* it anymore. An old building can remember being a warehouse and be a beautiful home now. It's not lying about its history. It's living in its new purpose.

Young women often carry the weight of "once a sinner, always a sinner." That's not biblical. You were a sinner. Now you're a saint. Literally. See yourself as God sees you: holy, blameless, His beloved—even when you stumble. Stumbling doesn't change your nature. It just means you need to confess and come back into the light with your Father.

### Day 10 - You are holy—set apart for God

**Main Scripture: 1 Peter 1:15–16**

"But as he which hath called you is holy, even so be ye holy in all manner of conversation; Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy."

You are holy. Not "become holy." "Be holy." This is your identity, not your assignment. You are set apart. Sanctified. Belonging to God. No longer common. No longer for worldly use.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Hebrews 10:10 — "By the which will we are sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all." Sanctified. Done. Not in progress. One offering, one time, all believers, all sins.

1 Corinthians 6:19–20 — "What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body." You are not your own. You were bought. You belong to God. Your body is His house.

Ephesians 1:3–4 — "Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ... According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love." Chosen. Holy. Before the world began, you were marked for holiness.

Leviticus 20:26 (in New Testament context) — "And ye shall be holy unto me: for I the Lord am holy, and have severed you from other people, that ye should be mine." Severed. Set apart. Made distinct. This is God's intention for His people.

Titus 2:14 — "Who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works." Peculiar. Set apart. You are God's peculiar treasure.

**Reality to Master:**

Holiness doesn't mean perfection. It means *set apart*. You are reserved for God. Your sexuality, your time, your energy, your loyalty—these are not neutral. They belong to the holy. You belong to the holy.

This is your identity, not your aspiration. You don't have to earn holiness. You have it. What you do is live from it. Make choices that honor the fact that you're set apart for God. Everything you do with your body, your time, your relationships either honors or dishonors the holiness you already are.

And here's the key: when you step away from holiness into darkness and sin, you separate yourself from God's presence—not because He abandoned you, but because you stepped out of the light. Like moving out of the sun into shadow. God didn't move. You did. And you can turn around and walk back into the light anytime.

**Important Notes:**

This is where Section 1 lands: You understand what Christ finished (Days 1–3). You understand the great exchange (Day 4). You understand your legal standing (Days 5–6). You understand your authority over sin (Day 7). You understand you're dead to your old way (Day 8). You understand you're someone new (Day 9). And now you understand: *you belong to God. You are His. Holy.*

This is not shame-based motivation ("I have to be good because God's watching"). This is identity-based freedom ("I *am* good because I belong to Good"). 

The consciousness builds: From forgiven → graced → clean → exchanged → justified → righteous → liberated → reborn → **holy**. Not works. Not striving. Just the settling in of who she actually is. And the understanding that when you stumble, God doesn't move. You can come back.

## Section 2: Peace & Reconciliation with God

**Central Truth:** You have peace with God—not as a feeling, but as a fact. The hostility between you and God is ended. You are at rest in His affection. When you separate yourself through sin, He calls you back. And your restoration is immediate and complete.

### Day 11 - Peace is not a feeling; it is the absence of hostility

**Main Scripture: Romans 5:1**

"Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ."

Notice the tense: "we *have* peace." Present possession. Not "we will have peace if we're good enough." You have it now. This is your current standing.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Luke 2:14 — "Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men." The angels did not proclaim absence of earthly war. They proclaimed peace between God and men. That enmity was ended.

Isaiah 53:5 — "The chastisement for our peace was upon him." The punishment that produces *shalom*—wholeness, wellness, right relationship—fell on Him entirely.

Ephesians 2:14–16 — "He himself is our peace... having abolished in his flesh the enmity." Christ destroyed the hostility between God and humanity. It's done.

Colossians 1:20 — "And, having made peace through the blood of his cross... to reconcile all things unto himself." Peace through reconciliation. The hostility is gone.

John 14:27 — "Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you." His peace is not dependent on circumstances.

**Reality to Master:**

Peace is not a feeling. It is not the absence of conflict around you. It is the **absence of hostility between you and God.** You are no longer at odds with Him. The enmity is gone. This is not something you earn or maintain through good behavior. This is your position in Christ.

God is not your enemy. God is not secretly mad at you. God is not hostile toward you. That ended at the cross.

**Important Notes:**

The world thinks peace is calm circumstances, no conflict, everything going smoothly. That's not what Scripture means by peace. Scripture means: you and God are no longer enemies. The relationship is restored. He is for you, not against you.

Many young women live as though they're in a cold war with God—trying to be good enough to win His favor, waiting for Him to finally be pleased with them. But peace says: The war is over. You won. Not because you were strong, but because Christ won. You have peace. Present tense. Right now.

### Day 12 - You are adopted—chosen, beloved, Father

**Main Scripture: Ephesians 1:4–5**

"According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love: Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will."

Before the world was made, God chose you. Not randomly. Not reluctantly. He predestined you for adoption. You were wanted.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Romans 8:15 — "For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father." You have a Father. Not a master. Not a judge. A Father. And you can call Him "Abba"—the intimate, familial word for Father.

1 John 3:1 — "Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God." What kind of love is this? The love of a Father who makes you His child.

Galatians 4:6–7 — "And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father. Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ." You are not a servant. You are a son/daughter. You are an heir.

John 1:12 — "But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name." You have the right, the legal standing, the power to be called His child.

Jeremiah 31:3 — "The Lord hath appeared of old unto me, saying, Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee." Everlasting love. Not conditional. Not earned. Chosen.

**Reality to Master:**

You are not God's reluctant servant. You are not trying to please a distant, disapproving judge. **You are His child. He chose you. He actually likes you.** This is not metaphorical. This is your actual relationship.

God is your Father in the truest sense of the word. The best father you can imagine—patient, protective, generous, proud of you, wanting the best for you—that is a shadow of who He is. He is even better.

He didn't adopt you because you deserved it or earned it. He adopted you because He wanted you. You were wanted before the world began.

**Important Notes:**

Many young women have broken relationships with their earthly fathers. Absent fathers, cold fathers, critical fathers, abusive fathers. The gospel invites you into a different story: **You have a Father who is present, warm, generous, and proud of you.** He is not waiting for you to be perfect. He is not secretly ashamed of you. He chose you.

This changes everything about how you approach God. You are not trying to earn love. You are living from love. You are not a servant working for approval. You are a daughter secure in your Father's affection.

### Day 13 - You have direct access to God

**Main Scripture: Ephesians 2:18**

"For through him we both have access by one Spirit unto the Father."

Direct. Unmediated. One Spirit. You do not need an intermediary. You do not need to go through anyone else. You have immediate, direct access to God the Father.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Hebrews 4:16 — "Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need." Come boldly. Not ashamed. Not hesitantly. Not through a gatekeeper. Directly to the throne.

Hebrews 10:19–22 — "Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus... Let us draw near with a true heart." The blood opened a way. You have boldness to enter. Not fear. Not shame. Boldness.

Romans 5:2 — "By whom also we have access by faith into this grace wherein we stand." You have access. Not someday. Now. Into grace. Into His presence.

1 Peter 3:18 — "For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God." Christ's purpose included bringing you to God. Direct. Not blocked. Not hindered.

John 14:6 — "Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me." Through Christ, you come directly to the Father.

**Reality to Master:**

You do not need a priest. You do not need a pastor to access God. You do not need to be "spiritual enough" or "good enough" or "have your act together enough." **You have direct access.** Right now. To the Father. To the throne. To grace.

This access is not blocked by your sin (Days 1-10 established that). It's not blocked by your weakness. It's not blocked by your past. You can come directly. Boldly.

**Important Notes:**

In many African churches and faith traditions, access to God is mediated—you need a pastor to pray for you, a prophet to hear from God, an elder to intercede. That's not biblical. That's control. You are a royal priesthood (1 Peter 2:9). You have direct access.

This is empowering: You don't have to wait for someone to be "available." You don't have to hope someone important will pray for you. You can go directly to the throne. Right now. And God will hear you.

### Day 14 - Your prayers are not low-grade; you have first-class treatment

**Main Scripture: Philippians 4:6–7**

"Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and your minds through Christ Jesus."

Your requests matter. They get God's attention. They matter enough that praying about them brings peace.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

1 John 5:14–15 — "And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us: And if we know that he hear us, whatsoever we ask, we know that we have the petitions that we desired of him." He hears you. Not as a low-priority request. He hears you and grants what you ask (according to His will).

Matthew 7:7–8 — "Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you: For every one that asketh receiveth." Ask. Seek. Knock. You will receive. This is not "maybe if you're spiritual enough." It's: you ask, you receive.

James 4:2–3 — "Ye have not, because ye ask not... Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss." The implication: if you ask right, you receive. Your prayers are not being ignored. They're being evaluated on merit, not on your status.

Psalm 27:10 — "When my father and my mother forsake me, then the Lord will take me up." Even your earthly parents fail. But your requests to your Father in heaven—they matter.

Proverbs 15:8 — "The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord: but the prayer of the upright is his delight." Your prayers delight Him. You are upright in Christ. Your prayers delight the Father.

**Reality to Master:**

Your prayers are not low-grade. You are not a second-class citizen in God's kingdom. You do not have to hope some "super Christian" prays for you to get God's attention. **When you pray, God listens. Your requests matter. You have first-class treatment.**

You are not waiting in line behind pastors, prophets, or "more spiritual" people. You have direct access. Your prayers go straight to the throne. And God delights in them.

**Important Notes:**

Many young women were taught: "Pray, but the pastor's prayers are stronger," or "You have to be more mature in faith for God to listen," or "Only prophets really hear from God." These are lies designed to keep you small.

Your prayers matter. God hears you. When you ask, according to His will, you receive. Not "maybe." Not "if you're worthy." You ask, you receive. This is your birthright as God's child.

### Day 15 - You are an insider; God does His work through you

**Main Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:20**

"Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ's stead, be ye reconciled to God."

You are an ambassador. Not a servant. Not a laborer. An ambassador. God's representative. And through you, God Himself beseeches people.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

1 Corinthians 12:12–13 — "For as the body is one, and hath many members... so also is Christ... For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body." You are a member of Christ's body. Not decoration. Not appendix. A functioning, necessary member.

Ephesians 4:11–12 — "And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; For the perfecting of the saints... for the edifying of the body of Christ." You have a function. You have gifts. You are instrumental to God's work.

John 14:12 — "Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do." The works Jesus did—healing, deliverance, mercy—you will do them. Not someday. "He... shall do."

2 Corinthians 6:1 — "We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain." You are a worker together with God. Not under Him. Together with Him.

1 Peter 4:10 — "As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God." You have gifts. Your job is to steward them and use them.

**Reality to Master:**

You are not a spectator in God's kingdom. You are not waiting for "real" Christians or leaders to do the work. **God does His healing through you. His deliverance through you. His mercy through you. His answers to other people's prayers—through you.**

You are a trusted insider in heaven. God trusts you with His work. He sends you as His ambassador. When people meet you and experience love, mercy, healing, deliverance—they're meeting God.

**Important Notes:**

Many young women think: "I'm just a young woman. The real work is done by pastors and prophets." But Scripture says the work is done through the body—through all believers. You are not marginal. You are central.

God doesn't need permission to use you. He doesn't need you to be "spiritual enough." He uses you as you are. Your compassion, your words, your presence—these are tools God uses to touch people.

This is humbling and empowering: God chooses to work through you. Not because you're special in yourself, but because you're His, and He delights to do His work through His people.

### Day 16 - He sees you as up there with Him, not as weak

**Main Scripture: Psalm 8:5**

"For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast crowned him with glory and honour."

God crowned you with glory and honor. He sees you as exalted, not diminished. This is how He views you.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Ephesians 2:6 — "And hath raised us up together, and made us sit together in heavenly places in Christ Jesus." You are seated in heavenly places. Not striving. Not groveling. Seated. At rest. In a position of authority.

1 John 4:17 — "As he is, so are we in this world." As Christ is, so are you. If Christ is confident before the Father, so are you. If Christ has authority, so do you.

Romans 8:37 — "Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us." Not survivors. Not barely hanging on. More than conquerors.

1 Peter 2:9 — "But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people." Royal. Priesthood. Holy. Chosen. This is how God categorizes you.

**Reality to Master:**

God does not see you as weak, incompetent, or unworthy. **He sees you as crowned with glory and honor. He sees you as seated in heavenly places. He sees you as royal.**

The proof? Look at the people He sent to do His work: Moses (stammered), Gideon (hiding in fear), Peter (just abandoned Jesus), Paul (persecuted Christians). He didn't say, "Wait until they're perfect." He said, "Go." Because He sees something in you that you haven't seen in yourself yet.

**Important Notes:**

When you hear God's voice calling you to do something—speak, lead, serve, step out—your first response is usually, "But I'm not [strong enough / smart enough / experienced enough / spiritual enough]." But that's insecurity, not humility. God sees you differently than you see yourself. He sees you as capable. As ready. As up there with Him.

### Day 17 - You have boldness—to speak and act for Him, and to speak to Him

**Main Scripture: Ephesians 3:12**

"In whom we have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of him."

Boldness. Confidence. Access. These are not things you earn through spiritual performance. These are yours in Christ. This is your default position.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

1 John 4:17 — "Herein is our love made perfect, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment: because as he is, so are we in this world." Perfect love produces boldness. God's perfect love toward you produces boldness in you.

Acts 4:29–31 — "And now, Lord, behold their threatenings: and grant unto thy servants, that with all boldness they may speak thy word... And when they had prayed, the place was shaken: and they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and they spake the word of God with boldness." Boldness to speak His word is something you can pray for and receive. It's available.

2 Timothy 1:7 — "For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind." You are not given a spirit of timidity. You are given power. Love. Soundness of mind.

Hebrews 10:35 — "Cast not away therefore your confidence, which hath great recompence of reward." Don't throw away your confidence. It has a reward.

Proverbs 28:1 — "The wicked flee when no one pursueth them: but the righteous are bold as a lion." You are righteous (Day 6). Therefore, you are bold as a lion.

**Reality to Master:**

You have boldness. Not arrogance. Not recklessness. Boldness—the confident assurance that you belong to God and He is with you.

This boldness means:
- **To speak for Him**: You can speak truth. You can share your faith. You don't have to whisper it. You can speak it boldly.
- **To act for Him**: You can step out in faith, believing God is with you. You don't have to wait for permission from leaders. You can act.
- **To speak to Him**: You can pray boldly, asking for what you need. You can speak your heart to your Father without fear.

**Important Notes:**

Many young women were taught to be quiet, compliant, small. "Know your place," they were told. But your place is with God. Seated with Christ in heavenly places. And from that place, you speak boldly.

This boldness is not about being loud or aggressive. It's about being unafraid. Unashamed. Secure in whose you are.

### Day 18 - Reconciliation: He calls you back; confession removes your barrier

**Main Scripture: 1 John 1:7–9**

"But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship one with another, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin... If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

This is the reconciliation passage. When you walk in light (transparency, honesty), you have fellowship. When you fall into darkness, confession brings you back into light.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Isaiah 59:1–2 — "Behold, the Lord's hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear: But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear." Sin creates distance. But God is listening for the moment you turn back.

Hosea 6:1 — "Come, and let us return unto the Lord: for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up." The imagery: God doesn't hide from you. He calls you to come back. And when you do, healing happens immediately.

Luke 15:20–21 — The prodigal father. The son comes back, and the father runs to meet him. There's no punishment, no lecture. Just immediate restoration.

Psalm 23:3 — "He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake." When you've wandered, He restores you. It's what He does.

Jeremiah 3:12 — "Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and say, Return, thou backsliding Israel... and I will not cause mine anger to fall upon you." God calls backsliders to return. His anger doesn't fall on you. His arms are open.

**Reality to Master:**

When you sin and separate yourself through darkness (unforgiveness, hardness, hiding), God doesn't go anywhere. **He calls you to come back.** Like the father in Luke 15, He's watching for your return.

The moment you confess—the moment you tell the truth about what you did—the barrier you created is removed. Completely. Immediately. Restoration is not "eventually." It's now.

God is not punishing you or making you grovel. He's welcoming you home.

**Important Notes:**

Many young women carry deep shame about sin and think: "I've gone too far. God won't take me back. I have to punish myself or prove I'm sorry." But that's not how reconciliation works with God.

He says: "Come. Tell me the truth. Let the light in. And I will cleanse you." Restoration is immediate. No waiting period. No probation. You confess, you're clean.

This is the opposite of how the world works. The world says, "You messed up. Now you have to earn your way back." God says, "You messed up. Come home. Now."

### Day 19 - God's punishment fell on Christ; consequences are real but not punitive

**Main Scripture: Isaiah 53:5**

"But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed."

Punishment. Chastisement. All of it fell on Him. Not on you.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Romans 8:1 — "There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus." No condemnation. Not in God's mind. Not in God's plan.

2 Corinthians 5:21 — "For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him." He took your sin. You got His righteousness. The exchange happened.

Hebrews 9:27–28 — "And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment: So Christ was once offered to bear the sins of many." Christ bore sins. Singular offering. For many. You are included.

1 Peter 2:24 — "Who his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree, that we, being dead to sins, should live unto righteousness: by whose stripes ye were healed." Healed. Not punished. Not paying a debt. Healed.

Galatians 3:13 — "Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us." The curse fell on Him. You are redeemed from it.

**Reality to Master:**

This is crucial: **God's punishment did not come to you. It came to Christ.** You will never experience God's wrath, His judgment, His punitive action. That's not your lot.

But natural consequences are real and important. When you:
- Commit fornication → a child may be born (not punishment; a new life)
- Steal → you may be arrested (not God punishing; law working)
- Abuse substances → your health deteriorates (not God punishing; body responding)
- Gossip → relationships break (not God punishing; relational consequence)

These consequences flow naturally from choices. They are not God ambushing you. They're how the world works. And wisdom means: understand that your choices matter and shape your reality.

**Important Notes:**

This distinction matters enormously in African contexts where Christians often think, "If bad things happen, God is punishing me for sin." But God's punishment fell on Christ. What you experience are natural consequences + God's grace helping you through.

Don't be confused: understanding that consequences are real doesn't mean you're being punished. It means your choices have weight. That's empowering. That's wisdom. It means your life is in your hands (with God's help), not at the whim of a punishing God.

The goal is not to avoid consequences (some are unavoidable). The goal is to make wise choices so you don't create unnecessary suffering for yourself.

### Day 20 - Grace super abounds; God turns your mistakes into wins

**Main Scripture: Romans 5:20**

"Moreover the law entered, that the offence might abound. But where sin abounded, grace did much more abound."

Where sin is great, grace is greater. Not equal. Greater. Surpassing. Super-abounding.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Romans 8:28 — "And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose." All things. Even your mistakes. Even your failures. God works them together for good.

Genesis 50:20 (Joseph) — "But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good." What was meant to harm him, God turned to good. This is God's specialty.

Joel 2:25 — "And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten, the cankerworm, and the caterpillar, and the palmerworm, my great army which I sent among you." Restoration. God restores what was lost through your mistakes.

Psalm 84:11 — "For the Lord God is a sun and shield: the Lord will give grace and glory: no good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly." Grace. Glory. Every good thing. This is His abundance toward you.

2 Corinthians 12:9 — "And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness." Your weakness, your failure—that's where grace super-abounds.

**Reality to Master:**

You will make mistakes. You will fail. You will stumble. And in those moments, grace doesn't just cover you—it *super-abounds*. It's excessive. Generous. Lavish.

More than that: God takes your mistakes and turns them into wins. Your failures become wisdom. Your stumbles become compassion (you understand what others go through). Your regrets become maturity.

This is not permission to sin carelessly. This is the reality of living under grace: God is for you. Even when you mess up. Especially when you mess up. He's right there, turning it into something good.

**Important Notes:**

This is where Section 2 lands: You are at peace with God. You are His beloved child. You have direct access and first-class treatment. You are an insider doing God's work. He sees you as capable and crowns you with honor. You speak boldly to Him and for Him. When you separate yourself through sin, He calls you back immediately. His punishment fell on Christ, not you. And through it all, grace super-abounds.

You are living under an economy of abundance, not scarcity. God is not rationing grace. He's not saying, "Well, you've used up your forgiveness quota." He's saying, "Where you think you've failed most, grace abounds most."

This is the peace that passes understanding: You are completely, lavishly, abundantly loved—not because of what you do, but because of who you are in Christ.

Main Scripture: 1 John 2:13–14

"...ye have overcome the wicked one."

Supporting Scriptures:
1 John 4:4
1 John 5:4–5
Revelation 12:11
Luke 10:19

Reality to master:
Overcoming Satan is not only future hope. Scripture says believers "have overcome" him.

Important notes:
This is direct perfect-tense language: "ye have overcome." The believer's task is to grow into the consciousness and practice of what is already true in Christ. We overcome by the blood, the Word, faith, and the indwelling Greater One.

### Day 40 — The Wicked One Touches Him Not

Main Scripture: 1 John 5:18

"We know that whosoever is born of God sinneth not... and that wicked one toucheth him not."

Supporting Scriptures:
John 1:12–13
2 Corinthians 5:17
1 Peter 1:23
James 4:7
1 John 3:8–9

Reality to master:
Freedom from Satan's dominion rests on new birth: you are no longer under the old master, old kingdom, or old identity.

Important notes:
The new birth changes spiritual jurisdiction. The believer belongs to God. This does not mean Satan never attempts attacks, temptations, or deception. It means he has no rightful ownership or dominion. The believer must submit to God, resist the devil, and stand in the reality of new birth.

## Free from curses

Galatians 3:13 — "Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law, having become a curse for us." The mechanics: substitution, one-time, complete. This covers Deuteronomy 28's full curse catalog — poverty, sickness, defeat.
Isaiah 53:4 (Hebrew: nasa and sabal) — He bore and carried our griefs and sorrows. These are load-bearing verbs. The weight was transferred.

## Provision / Material Redemption

2 Corinthians 8:9 — "Though he was rich, yet for your sakes he became poor, that you through his poverty might become rich." Same exchange grammar as 5:21. Substitution completed.
Galatians 3:13–14 — Redemption from the curse that the blessing of Abraham might come. Abraham's blessing is materially thick (Genesis 12, 17, 22) — fruitfulness, increase, inheritance.
Philippians 4:19 — "My God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus." The basis is "his riches in glory" — what Christ possesses by virtue of his finished work.

## Eternal Life / Resurrection

John 5:24 — "Has passed from death into life." Perfect tense again. The believer is already on the other side of judgment.
Ephesians 2:5–6 — "Made us alive together with Christ... raised us up together... seated us together in the heavenly places." Three aorist verbs. The ascension of the believer is a completed positional reality.
Colossians 3:3 — "You died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God." The death happened; the life is already secured.

## You have Authority

### Day 9 - You are a new creation—new identity, new person

**Main Scripture: 2 Corinthians 5:17**

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

You are not improved. You are not reformed. You are *new*. The old person—her failures, her story, her shame—is passed away. You are not that person anymore.

**Supporting Scriptures:**

Ephesians 4:22–24 — "That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; And be renewed in the spirit of your mind; And that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness." You have a new self, created in righteousness and holiness.

Colossians 3:9–10 — "Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds; And have put on the new man, which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him." The old man is *put off*—removed like old clothes. The new man is *put on*.

John 1:12–13 — "But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name: Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God." You were born again. Different parentage. Different nature.

1 Peter 1:23 — "Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever." You have an incorruptible nature now—not based on flesh, genetics, or family patterns.

1 John 3:1–2 — "Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God... Now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him." You are a daughter of God. Now. Not becoming. Now.

**Reality to Master:**

Your old identity is finished. The person who was bound by shame, driven by sin, ruled by fear—that person does not exist anymore. She died and rose new.

This doesn't mean your *memories* disappear or your *history* rewrites. It means your *identity* changes. You are no longer "that girl who did those things." You are a daughter of God, born of His nature, bearing His image.

When the enemy whispers your old story ("Remember what you were?"), the answer is: "That was her. I'm new."

**Important Notes:**

This is the hardest thing to believe for women with heavy pasts. The mind says, "But I remember what I did. I can't just be new." But being new doesn't erase memory—it reframes identity. You can remember something and not *be* it anymore. An old building can remember being a warehouse and be a beautiful home now. It's not lying about its history. It's living in its new purpose.

Young women often carry the weight of "once a sinner, always a sinner." That's not biblical. You were a sinner. Now you're a saint. Literally. See yourself as God sees you: holy, blameless, His beloved.
